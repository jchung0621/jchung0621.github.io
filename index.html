Biography

My name is Jason Chung, and I'm a senior student at UCLA studying Statistics and Data Science. I am interested in the intersection of data science and finance. I'm expected to graduate in summer of 2026 with Bachelor of Science in Statistics and Data Science.

Why Finance?

I immigrated from South Korea to United States back in 2017. Due to language barrier my parents weren't able to understand any official documents. I've developed a strong sense of responsibility in finance and tax so naturally I chose to specialize in finance.

My Experience

I've gained experience in Data Science through Student Organization such as Bruin Sports Analytics (BSA) and gained knowledge in finance as finance officer for Soon Movement Global (SMG). I have built up strong knowledge and skill in both fields, and I aspire to find my career in these fields.

Projects
