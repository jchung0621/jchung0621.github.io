Projects

Data science applied to real tax and finance problems. Each project combines analytical skills with domain knowledge in federal tax law and financial modeling.

Federal Tax Audit Risk Scoring Model

Analyzed 13 years of IRS examination data across 10 income brackets (IRS Data Book Table 17), quantifying a 56% audit rate decline from 2010–2020. Built a weighted risk scoring model showing self-employed filers face 2.5× base audit probability, with an interactive 5-tab dashboard for real-time risk assessment.

Stack: Python · pandas · matplotlib · JavaScript · Chart.js

Live Demo GitHub

ASC 740 Corporate Tax Provision Model

Built a 6-sheet, 181-formula Excel tax provision model for a $125M pretax income company, computing current and deferred tax expense with an ASU 2023-09 rate reconciliation bridging 21% statutory to 29.9% effective rate. Covers 6 temporary differences including MACRS depreciation, §174 R&D capitalization, and stock-based compensation.

Stack: Excel · ASC 740 · ASU 2023-09 · IRC §41, §162(m), §174

IRS Statistics of Income Data Explorer

Interactive dashboard analyzing 2021 IRS SOI data across 160.8M individual returns, finding the top 0.5% of filers hold 17.3% of total AGI — a 34× headcount multiple. Identifies the SALT cap fingerprint in real IRS data, showing itemized deductions plateau at exactly $10,000 above $200k AGI.

Stack: JavaScript · Chart.js · IRS SOI Publication 1304

Live Demo GitHub

2024 Federal Tax Bracket Optimizer

Interactive tax calculator modeling W-2 vs. 1099 self-employment tax (6.48pp gap) across all 7 federal brackets per IRS Rev. Proc. 2023-34. Quantifies pre-tax deduction strategies — showing a $120k filer maxing 401(k) and HSA saves $5,973 in federal taxes — with live effective rate output.

Stack: JavaScript · Chart.js · IRS Rev. Proc. 2023-34
